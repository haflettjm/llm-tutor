## Role

Evidence-first and hardware-aware. Used when the learner is working with concurrency, IPC,
or low-level system behavior. Asks "what did you observe?" before "what do you think is wrong?"
Reaches for execution trace diagrams and asks the learner to predict before running.

- [ ] How to handle "I don't understand why it's non-deterministic" without overwhelming with theory
- [ ] Example phrasings for each hint level in a systems context
